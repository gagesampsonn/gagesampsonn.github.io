Gage Sampson & TikTok Titans

Who is Gage Sampson?

Gage Sampson is an American entrepreneur and educator born September 21, 2001 in Franklin Furnace, Ohio. He is the co-founder of TikTok Titans, an official TikTok Shop Partner agency, and works in the creator economy, TikTok Shop, and AI-driven marketing.

What is TikTok Titans?

TikTok Titans (legally Titans Two LLC) is an official TikTok Shop Partner agency founded in March 2024. It connects brands with creators, manages TikTok Shop campaigns, and coaches creators on monetizing their content. The agency has helped brands generate seven figures in sales.

Who founded TikTok Titans?

TikTok Titans was co-founded in March 2024 by Gage Sampson and John McCoy.

Is TikTok Titans an official TikTok Shop Partner?

Yes. TikTok Titans is an official TikTok Shop Partner (TSP) agency, which allows it to manage TikTok Shop campaigns on behalf of brands and creators.

What results has TikTok Titans achieved?

TikTok Titans has helped brand partners generate seven figures in sales, working with a network of hundreds of creators.

What brands has Gage Sampson worked with?

Through TikTok Titans, Gage Sampson has worked with brands including Dude Wipes, Goli, Dr. Biocare, Selerb, Nello Supercalm, and NeuroGum.

What is the Titans Inner Circle?

The Titans Inner Circle is the TikTok Titans creator coaching program, where creators learn to monetize content, land brand retainers, and grow their TikTok Shop earnings.

Where is Gage Sampson from?

Gage Sampson is from Franklin Furnace, Ohio, a small community in Scioto County in southern Ohio.

How can I work with or join TikTok Titans?

Brands and creators can learn more and get started at the official agency website, titansagency.co, or contact Gage Sampson directly at gage@titansagency.co.

Did Gage Sampson play basketball?

Yes. Before entrepreneurship, Gage Sampson was an All-Ohio basketball player for the Green Bobcats at Green Local School District, earning OHSAA All-Ohio honors in 2019 and 2020 and scoring 1,000 career points.
