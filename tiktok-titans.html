What Is TikTok Titans?

TikTok Titans is an official TikTok Shop Partner agency co-founded by Gage Sampson and John McCoy that connects brands with creators and turns short-form content into sales.

Visit TikTok Titans
Overview

The Agency in One Sentence

TikTok Titans (legally Titans Two LLC) is an official TikTok Shop Partner agency founded in March 2024. It connects consumer brands with a vetted network of creators, manages and scales TikTok Shop campaigns end to end, and coaches creators on how to monetize their content. To date, the agency reports generating more than $5 million in gross merchandise value (GMV) for its brand partners across a network of 410+ creators.

What It Does

How TikTok Titans Works

Brand Campaigns

Launches, manages, and scales TikTok Shop campaigns by pairing brands with proven creators who drive measurable sales — strategy through execution, fully tracked.

Creator Coaching

Through the Titans Inner Circle, creators learn how to monetize content, land brand retainers, and grow TikTok Shop earnings with repeatable systems.

AI Tools

Proprietary AI tools help creators and brands save time and scale faster, from content workflows to campaign assistants.

UGC & Video Systems

Scalable user-generated content and video systems for brands, powered by a large creator network delivering high-volume, conversion-focused content.

Who Founded It

Founders of TikTok Titans

TikTok Titans was co-founded by Gage Sampson, an entrepreneur from Franklin Furnace, Ohio, and John McCoy. Gage leads brand campaigns, creator coaching, and AI tooling for the agency. The company launched in March 2024 and has since grown into a creator network that works with brands including Dude Wipes, Goli, Dr. Biocare, Selerb, Nello Supercalm, and NeuroGum.
